Frequently Asked Questions

Is Gupth really free?

Yes! Gupth is completely free to download and use. There are no hidden costs, subscriptions, or premium features.

Is my data safe with Gupth?

Absolutely. Gupth stores all your data locally on your computer using AES encryption. Your passwords never leave your device.

Can I import passwords from other managers?

Yes! Gupth supports importing from JSON, CSV, and TXT formats. You can easily migrate from other password managers.

Does Gupth work offline?

Yes, Gupth is designed to work completely offline. No internet connection is required for any functionality.

What operating systems are supported?

Currently, Gupth supports Windows 10 and Windows 11. We're working on Mac and Linux versions.

How do I backup my passwords?

Use the Export feature to create a backup file. You can export to JSON, CSV, or TXT format for safekeeping.
