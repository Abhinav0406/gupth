About Gupth

Gupth was created with one simple principle: your passwords should be completely under your control. In a world where data breaches are common and privacy is increasingly rare, Gupth provides a secure, local solution for password management.

Unlike cloud-based password managers, Gupth stores all your data locally on your computer. This means no internet connection is required, no data is sent to external servers, and you have complete control over your sensitive information.
